Local Transcribe privacy policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Local Transcribe is a Windows app that turns audio and video files into text and subtitle files. It runs entirely on your computer.

What the app does with your files

  • Files you add are read from your disk, transcribed on your own CPU or graphics card, and the results are written next to the original file.
  • No audio, video, text, file names, or usage data are sent to us or to anyone else. The app has no account and no server of ours to talk to.

The one network connection

On first use of a speech model the app downloads that model once from Hugging Face, a public model host, and stores it under your Windows user profile (%LOCALAPPDATA%\LocalTranscribe\models). This request contains no information about you or your files beyond what any download does (your IP address to the host). After that, the app works fully offline.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data, content, or analytics. Purchases are handled by the Microsoft Store, and we never see your payment details.
